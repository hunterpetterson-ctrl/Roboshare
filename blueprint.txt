1. The Vision: A Living CensusWe are continuously surrounded by new inhabitants—delivery rovers, patrol drones, and autonomous shuttles—yet we know almost nothing about them.BOTdar flips the script. It is something that will be of utility; it is a community layer for the physical world. We are building the &quot;Bot Index&quot;—a free, living metric of robot population density that anyone can access. It turns the passive act of seeing a robot into an active, fun moment of discovery. By gamifying the &quot;scan,&quot; we crowd-source a real-time map of our machine neighbors.2. Core Pillars (The &quot;Big Three&quot;)These are the non-negotiable features. If we cut everything else, these three must remain.I. The Pulse (Visibility)Concept: A real-time &quot;heat map&quot; of activity, not just traffic updates.Experience: Users see where the robot population is migrating. Is your neighborhood a &quot;hotspot&quot; today? The map glows with activity, showing the density of machines in the area.Key Mechanic: The &quot;Bot Index&quot; Score. A simple number (0-100) showing how active the autonomous population is in your zone right now.II. The Discovery (Identity)Concept: &quot;Gotta spot 'em all.&quot;Experience: One-tap camera access. Point your phone at a machine, and our AI identifies its class (e.g., &quot;Sidewalk Courier Class-A&quot; or &quot;Quad-Legged Inspector&quot;).Key Mechanic: Collection &amp; Badges. Users earn &quot;Spotter&quot; badges for identifying new types of bots or contributing to the census.III. The Vibe (Community)Concept: Neighborhood Notes.Experience: Instead of just safety warnings, users leave &quot;Vibe Checks.&quot; Is this bot polite? Is it playing music? Is it stuck in a funny way?Key Mechanic: Community Threads. A feed attached to specific zones or bot types where neighbors can discuss the &quot;local wildlife.&quot;  Stronger together “Are you seeing what I’m seeing?l” 3.
